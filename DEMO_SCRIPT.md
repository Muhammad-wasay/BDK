# BinaDarKe (بغیر ڈر کے) • Live Demo Script
> **Target Duration**: Under 2 Minutes (1:55 Total)  
> **Speaker Role**: Presenter / Product Lead  
> **Audience**: Build4Better Hackathon Judges (NED University)

---

## ⏱️ Second-by-Second Presentation Script

### 0:00 – 0:12 | The User & The Root Problem
* **Screen**: Landing page with Abba Ji persona card visible.
* **Speaker**:
  > *"Assalam-o-Alaikum! Meet Abba Ji, a 67-year-old retired clerk. When receiving a message saying 'Your account will be blocked' or 'You won Rs. 50,000', he doesn't lack intelligence—he lacks a safe place to practice.
  > In real banking apps, one wrong tap costs real money. Telling elders 'don't click links' doesn't work. They need practice without fear. That is **BinaDarKe**."*

---

### 0:12 – 0:42 | Digital Ustaad: The Practice Phone
* **Action**: Click **"Practice Phone"** from the navigation bar. Select **"Check Payment Recipient"** in **Guided Mode**.
* **Screen**: Simulated Android phone interface with persistent green banner: *"PRACTICE ONLY: nothing is sent"*.
* **Speaker**:
  > *"First, our data-driven Practice Phone. Notice the persistent safety banner: nothing real is ever sent.
  > In Guided Mode, a gentle spotlight shows Abba Ji how to verify a recipient's title in a simulated EasyPaisa wallet before sending money.
  > If he taps the wrong button, there is zero shaming—just a calm hint. In Independent Mode, learners attempt the task unguided, building real-world muscle memory."*

---

### 0:42 – 1:05 | Scam Inspection & The Teach-Back Loop
* **Action**: Click **"Solo Test"**. Question 1 appears (Bank Account Block Threat from a 0345 mobile number). Tap **"Show Clue"**, then tap **"Dhoka Hai (Scam)"**. The Teach-Back card appears; tap the correct reason.
* **Speaker**:
  > *"Next, our 4-stage Teaching Loop across 15 Pakistani scenarios.
  > Here is an SMS threatening an account freeze within 24 hours. Abba Ji can listen in Urdu or Roman Urdu. If unsure, he taps 'Show Clue' to see why 11-digit mobile senders are suspicious.
  > When he flags it as a scam, we don't just say 'Correct'—we activate **Teach-Back**. He taps the reason why: 'Real banks never threaten blocks from ordinary mobile numbers or ask for OTPs.' This teaches enduring mental models."*

---

### 1:05 – 1:25 | Branching Call Simulator & Rule-Based Scanner
* **Action**: Navigate to **"Phone Call"**. Accept the incoming call from "+92 300 1234567" (HBL Verification Desk). Choose *"Arey foran rokein!"*, then on the second branch choose *"Nahi, main phone par code nahi bataunga"*.
* **Speaker**:
  > *"Elders frequently face phone impersonators. In our Branching Phone Call Simulator, Abba Ji hears a simulated bank manager demanding his OTP.
  > If he panicked and entered the code in simulation, he sees the consequence safely: 'Paise nikal gaye!'
  > But when he refuses to give the OTP, the scammer hangs up in frustration. Consequence-free practice replaces fear with confidence."*

---

### 1:25 – 1:42 | Result, Safe Citizen Certificate & Kid View
* **Action**: Navigate to **"Results"**. Show the 15-scenario breakdown, open **"Kid View"**, and highlight the WhatsApp Family Challenge.
* **Screen**: Result screen with confidence meter, Kid View modal, and certificate print dialog.
* **Speaker**:
  > *"When the learner finishes, they receive a personalized Safe Citizen Certificate.
  > Even better, we built **Kid View**: a zero-data summary designed for sons and daughters. It shows what Abba Ji practiced, alongside 3 calm conversation starters to chat about over evening chai.
  > With one tap, he can send a WhatsApp Family Challenge to his children or relatives."*

---

### 1:42 – 2:00 | Honest Engineering & Privacy Architecture
* **Action**: Open the **Privacy Policy** screen. Tap the Buzurg Mode toggle to show enlarged typography.
* **Speaker**:
  > *"To conclude with complete engineering honesty:
  > BinaDarKe is **100% client-side**. Zero backend, zero telemetry, zero cookies, zero external API dependencies. All simulated accounts and phone calls are completely mocked.
  > Built with React 19, Tailwind v4, and Vitest, it works offline as a PWA on budget Pakistani phones.
  > BinaDarKe empowers our parents to explore the digital world without fear. Thank you!"*
