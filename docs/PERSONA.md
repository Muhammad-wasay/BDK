## Persona: Meet Abba Ji (Fictional Composite Persona, Not a Real Person)

```
       ┌────────────────────────┐
       │      👴 Abba Ji        │
       │  Age: 67 • Rawalpindi  │
       │  Retired Govt Clerk    │
       └────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Demographics & Context
* **Name**: Abba Ji (fictional persona representing Pakistani parents and elders)
* **Age**: 65–70 years old
* **Location**: Urban / peri-urban Pakistan (Rawalpindi / Lahore / Karachi)
* **Education & Language**: Matriculated / FA. Reads Urdu comfortably; reads English bank terms with hesitation. Prefers listening to spoken explanations over deciphering dense text on a screen.

### 2. Device & Hardware Context
* **Primary Phone**: Budget Android device (Infinix Hot 12 / Tecno Spark / Samsung Galaxy A13) with 3GB RAM.
* **Screen State**: System font size set to Large; high screen brightness; slightly cracked tempered glass screen protector.
* **Connectivity**: 4G mobile data with occasional drop-offs, home Wi-Fi shared with grandchildren.

### 3. Everyday Apps Used
* **WhatsApp**: 90% of screen time. Listens to voice notes, receives family pictures, forwards daily morning greetings to family groups.
* **EasyPaisa / JazzCash**: Uses occasionally to pay electricity/gas bills or receive allowances sent by children living abroad or in other cities.
* **YouTube**: Religious lectures, gardening, and daily news bulletins.
* **Phone Dialer**: Traditional calling with contacts saved under descriptive names ("Bara Beta", "Doctor Sahab").

### 4. Deepest Fears & Emotional Barriers
1. **Fear of Financial Loss**: *"Agar ghalti se koi button dab gaya to meri saari pension kat jayegi."* (Fear that a single wrong tap will empty life savings).
2. **Fear of Scolding & Embarrassment**: Terrified of telling younger family members when something strange appears on screen, fearing being scolded: *"Aapko kitni dafa samjhaya hai phone mat chhera karein."*
3. **Artificial Urgency Vulnerability**: When a caller says *"Aapka account 10 minute mein band ho raha hai"*, panic overrides logical hesitation.
4. **Reluctance to Ask for Help**: Suffers in silence or asks a neighbor or shopkeeper instead of family, increasing vulnerability to offline and online scams.

### 5. Design Decisions in BinaDarKe Serving Abba Ji
* **Zero Real Risk Sandbox**: Every simulation clearly states *"Practice only: nothing real is sent"*.
* **Buzurg Mode**: 18px–22px text, high contrast outlines (#1B2440), and minimum 48px touch targets for trembling or less agile hands.
* **Spoken Audio Assistance**: Integrated Urdu Voiceover bar so Abba Ji can listen at a calm, natural pace (0.85x rate).
* **Respectful Tone**: Respectful Urdu copy with "Aap" and simple Pakistani cultural idioms, free of condescending jargon.
* **Forgiving Navigation**: Accidental back buttons lose nothing; one-tap retry; no punishment or shaming.
