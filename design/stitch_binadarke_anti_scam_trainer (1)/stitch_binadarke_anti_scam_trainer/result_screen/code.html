Bijli Bill Scam Ka Sabaq

Bijli, Gas, ya Paani ke idaray kabhi bhi SMS ya WhatsApp par "Foran 40% discount payein" jaisa link nahi bhejtay. Jab bhi discount ya meter katne ki dhamki aayegi, hamesha apne official counter ya bank app se tasdeeq karein!

⭐ Golden Rule:

Agar koi kahe "Abhi link khol kar adaigi karo warna 2 ghante mein connection khatam", to wo 100% fraud hai.
